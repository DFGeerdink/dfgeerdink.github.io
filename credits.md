# Credits

This website builds on the work of others. Thank you!

## Terminal

The [Terminal](/terminal/) page is based on the CodePen [Resizable Terminal + shell.js css](https://codepen.io/jcubic/pen/WZvYGj) by [Jakub T. Jankiewicz](https://jcubic.pl/me). The terminal itself is powered by [jQuery Terminal](https://terminal.jcubic.pl) ([source on GitHub](https://github.com/jcubic/jquery.terminal)), also created by Jakub T. Jankiewicz and released under the MIT license.

The typing animation in the terminal is adapted from the CodePen [New Typing animation API](https://codepen.io/jcubic/pen/QWgwVVq) by Jakub T. Jankiewicz, with the boot sequence taken from [huntergregal.com](http://huntergregal.com/).

The window styling comes from [Shell.js](https://github.com/davidecaruso/shell.js) by Davide Caruso (MIT), and dragging and resizing are handled by [jQuery UI](https://jqueryui.com) (MIT).

## Molecules

The 3D molecules on the [Molecules](/molecules/) page, and the ones you can spawn on the [Terminal](/terminal/) page, are based on [Pure CSS Animated 3D Molecules (alpha)](https://codepen.io/thebabydino/pen/DzzjNd) by [Ana Tudor](https://codepen.io/thebabydino), who built every atom and molecule with nothing but CSS 3D transforms.
